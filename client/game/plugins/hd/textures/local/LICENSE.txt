Source assets, direct map URLs, material assignments and output hashes are recorded
in sources.json. The bundled files are resized and encoded for this renderer;
normal vectors are softened, renormalised and converted to its 117 HD convention.
Natural ground albedo is neutralised because terrain already supplies its hue.
